Mold Testing & Remediation

Mold follows moisture. Where there's been a leak or flood, spores can take hold inside 24–48 hours — often behind walls where you can't see them. We test to find it, contain it so it can't spread, remove it safely, and verify the air is clear before we close the wall back up.

Call 415-413-0066

Our Mold Process

  • Air and surface testing to locate every spore source
  • Containment barriers and negative air to protect clean areas
  • HEPA filtration and safe removal of mold-affected materials
  • Treatment of framing and surfaces to stop regrowth
  • Post-remediation testing to confirm the problem is gone
  • Rebuild of removed drywall, trim, and finishes

Questions

Frequently Asked

How do I know if I have mold?

Musty odor, visible staining, and recent water damage are the common signs — but much mold is hidden. Air testing gives you a definitive answer.

Is mold dangerous?

It can aggravate allergies and respiratory issues, especially for sensitive individuals. Prompt professional remediation is the safe path.

Do you test after removal?

Always. Post-remediation verification testing is how we confirm the space is safe before reconstruction.
