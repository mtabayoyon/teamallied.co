One Company, Demo Through Done

Most restoration jobs involve two phases: mitigation (stopping and cleaning up the damage) and reconstruction (rebuilding what was removed). Many companies only do one — leaving you to find and coordinate a separate contractor while your home sits half-finished.

Allied does both. The same team that extracted the water and removed the damaged materials rebuilds the space, so nothing falls through the cracks between contractors and your project actually gets finished.

Repair & Rebuild Services

  • Drywall removal, replacement, texture & paint
  • Flooring repair and replacement
  • Trim, baseboard, and finish carpentry
  • Cabinetry and fixture reinstallation
  • Structural repairs to framing and subfloor
  • Full-room and whole-home reconstruction after major loss

Why One Crew Matters

  • No coordinating multiple contractors

  • Accountability start to finish
