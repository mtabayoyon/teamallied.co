Now Hiring — Join the Allied Team

We're a growing Bay Area restoration company looking for dependable, hard-working people who take pride in helping families and businesses recover.

Why Work at Allied

Restoration work matters. When you join Allied, you're the person who shows up when someone's having one of the worst days of their year — and you're the one who helps make it right. That's meaningful work, and we treat the people who do it that way.

As a regional company, we offer the stability of established operations with the close-knit culture of a team that actually knows each other. We invest in training and certification, and we promote from within.

Roles We Hire For

  • Restoration & water damage technicians
  • Mold remediation crew
  • Reconstruction & finish carpenters
  • Crew leads & project coordinators
  • Drivers & equipment handlers

What We Look For

  • Reliability — you show up and follow through
  • A valid driver's license (for most roles)
  • Willingness to learn and earn certifications
  • Physical capability for jobsite work
  • A customer-first attitude in people's homes
