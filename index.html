Who Is Allied?

A Regional Company That Takes Every Loss Personally

We are a full-service restoration company specializing in restoration of structures due to water damage. We help mitigate mold damage in homes, as well as contain and remediate mold.

With our clients' needs in mind, we provide diligent, cost-effective solutions to water and mold damage in San Francisco. We continually develop and update our methods to meet new challenges while still satisfying the needs of our clients and all regulatory requirements.

Our experienced, qualified staff will guide you through each step — from consulting and providing estimates through the full restoration of your property. We're unique in that we're a small, regional company that prides itself on personal service.
