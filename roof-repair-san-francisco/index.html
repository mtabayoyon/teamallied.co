Roof Repair in San Francisco

Most interior water damage starts at the roof. A small leak during the rainy season can soak insulation, stain ceilings, and feed mold long before you notice a drip. Because Allied handles both the roof and the damage underneath it, we fix the cause and the symptom in one coordinated job.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Can you stop a leak during a storm?

We provide emergency tarping and board-up to stop active water intrusion, then complete permanent repairs once conditions allow.

Do you fix the interior damage too?

Yes. That's our advantage — one company repairs the roof and restores the water-damaged interior underneath it.

How do I know where the leak is?

Water travels, so the stain is rarely under the leak. We trace it to the actual source rather than guessing.
