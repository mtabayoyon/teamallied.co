Wood Floor Drying Services

Hardwood floors are often the most expensive casualty of a water loss — and the most savable. Cupping and buckling don't always mean replacement. With specialized in-place drying systems applied quickly, we routinely rescue floors that would otherwise be torn out.

Call 415-413-0066

How We Save Wood Floors

  • Moisture testing to measure saturation in boards and subfloor
  • Specialized floor-drying mat systems that pull water from within
  • Controlled dehumidification to dry without over-drying
  • Daily monitoring and documentation of moisture levels
  • Assessment of which boards are salvageable vs. replaceable
  • Refinishing guidance once the floor is fully dry

Questions

Frequently Asked

My floors are cupping — are they ruined?

Not necessarily. Cupping is often reversible if we start drying quickly. Many cupped floors flatten back out as moisture leaves.

How long does wood floor drying take?

It varies with saturation, but specialized systems dramatically speed it up versus air movers alone. We monitor daily until readings are normal.

Is drying cheaper than replacing?

Usually, yes — and it preserves the original floor. We'll tell you honestly when a floor is beyond saving.
