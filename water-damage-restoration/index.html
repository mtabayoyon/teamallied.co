Water Damage Restoration in San Francisco

Burst pipes, failed water heaters, storm flooding, and slow leaks all share one enemy: time. The longer water sits, the more it spreads into subfloors, drywall, and framing — and the closer it gets to permanent damage and mold. Allied's 24/7 crews stop the clock.

What We Do on a Water Loss

  • Emergency water extraction from floors, carpet, and subfloor
  • Moisture mapping and thermal imaging to find hidden water
  • Professional structural drying with air movers and dehumidifiers
  • Antimicrobial treatment to prevent mold growth
  • Removal of unsalvageable drywall, flooring, and insulation
  • Full reconstruction back to pre-loss condition

Questions

Frequently Asked

How fast can you respond?

Our line is staffed 24/7 and we dispatch emergency crews as quickly as possible across the Bay Area. Fast response is the single biggest factor in limiting water damage.

Do you work with my insurance?

Yes. We document the loss thoroughly — moisture readings, photos, and scope — in the format insurers expect, and can bill directly in most cases.

Will I need to replace my floors?

Not always. Rapid drying often saves hardwood and subfloor. We assess salvageability before removing anything.
