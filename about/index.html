About Allied Restoration Company

A full-service restoration company that's big enough to handle any loss — and small enough to treat yours like it matters.

Our Story

Allied Restoration Company was founded in 2002 and expanded to San Francisco in 2010. Since then we've grown into a multi-market Bay Area restoration and equipment platform — while holding onto the hands-on, personal service that set us apart from day one.

We are a full-service restoration company specializing in the restoration of structures damaged by water. We help mitigate mold damage in homes, as well as contain and remediate mold. With our clients' needs in mind, we provide diligent, cost-effective solutions to water and mold damage.

We continually develop and update our methods to meet new challenges while still satisfying the needs of our clients and all regulatory requirements. Our experienced, qualified, expert staff will guide you through each step of the process — from consulting and providing estimates of the damage through the restoration of your property.

We're unique in that we are a small, regional company which prides itself on personal service. When you call Allied, you're not a claim number — you're a neighbor.
